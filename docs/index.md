## 📖 About This Space

This knowledge base is maintained by the team and serves as the single source of truth for institutional knowledge. It's designed to:

- Reduce onboarding time for new team members
- Standardize practices across teams
- Document architectural decisions and patterns
- Capture tribal knowledge
- Enable self-service for common questions
